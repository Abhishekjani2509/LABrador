MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the date
of last signature below (the "Effective Date") by and between Continuum
Cloud Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation with its principal place of
business at 900 Congress Avenue, Suite 500, Austin, TX 78701 ("Provider"),
and Solano Bio Analytics, Inc., a Delaware corporation with its principal
place of business at 4400 Bayshore Parkway, Suite 200, San Mateo, CA 94403
("Client"). Provider and Client are each a "Party" and collectively the
"Parties."

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Provider offers a cloud-based data analytics and pipeline
management platform together with related implementation, configuration,
and support services; and

WHEREAS, Client desires to procure such services on the terms set forth
herein and in one or more Order Forms referencing this Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Order Form" means a document, executed by both Parties or accepted
electronically, that references this Agreement and specifies the Services,
fees, and Initial Term.
1.2 "Services" means the Continuum platform subscription and the
implementation, configuration, and support services described in an Order
Form or accompanying Statement of Work ("SOW").
1.3 "Deliverables" means any reports, dashboards, integrations, data
models, custom configurations, or other work product created or provided
by Provider in the course of performing the Services.
1.4 "Background IP" means any technology, tools, templates, methodologies,
or code owned or developed by a Party prior to, or independent of, this
Agreement.

2. SERVICES
2.1 Provider will perform the Services described in each Order Form in a
professional and workmanlike manner consistent with generally accepted
industry standards.
2.2 Client will provide reasonable cooperation, timely access to data and
personnel, and any materials reasonably requested by Provider to perform
the Services.

3. TERM
3.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues, together
with each Order Form issued hereunder, for the Initial Term set forth in
the applicable Order Form (the "Initial Term"), unless earlier terminated
as provided in Section 9.
3.2 Additional terms governing renewal and termination of this Agreement
and any Order Form are set forth in Section 9 and Section 11.

4. FEES AND PAYMENT
4.1 Client will pay the fees set forth in each Order Form (the "Fees").
Except as otherwise stated in an Order Form, Provider will invoice Client
monthly in advance for subscription Fees and monthly in arrears for
time-and-materials professional services.
4.2 Client shall pay each undisputed invoice within ninety (90) days of
the invoice date. Any amount not paid when due will accrue interest at the
lesser of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate
permitted by law, and Provider may, upon ten (10) days' written notice,
suspend access to the Services until all past-due amounts are paid in
full. If any invoice remains unpaid more than thirty (30) days past its
due date, all remaining Fees for the balance of the then-current Term
shall become immediately due and payable.
4.3 Client shall be responsible for all sales, use, and similar taxes
associated with the Services, excluding taxes based on Provider's net
income.
4.4 Fee Adjustments. Provider's standard subscription Fees are fixed
during the Initial Term. Commencing with the first Renewal Term (as
defined in Section 11.7) and upon each subsequent renewal, Provider may
increase the Fees by providing written notice at least thirty (30) days
prior to the start of the applicable Renewal Term. Continued use of the
Services following the effective date of any Fee increase constitutes
Client's acceptance of the revised Fees.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
5.1 Each Party may disclose confidential information to the other in
connection with this Agreement ("Confidential Information"). The
receiving Party will use the disclosing Party's Confidential Information
solely to perform its obligations hereunder and will protect it using at
least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential
information of similar nature, but no less than reasonable care.
5.2 Confidential Information does not include information that is or
becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party, was
rightfully known prior to disclosure, or is independently developed
without reference to the disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
6.1 As between the Parties, Provider retains all right, title, and
interest in and to the Services, the Continuum platform, and all
Background IP, including any updates, enhancements, or derivative works
thereof.
6.2 Any Deliverables, custom configurations, integrations, data models,
dashboards, or other work product created by Provider in the course of
performing the Services — including any Background IP of either Party
incorporated therein — shall be and remain the sole and exclusive
property of Provider. Client is granted a non-exclusive, non-transferable,
revocable license to use the Deliverables solely in connection with its
authorized use of the Services during the Term.
6.3 Client hereby assigns to Provider all right, title, and interest in
any feedback, suggestions, or configuration data Client provides in
connection with the Services, and Provider may use and incorporate the
foregoing into its products and services without restriction or
compensation to Client.

7. WARRANTIES; DISCLAIMER
7.1 Provider warrants that the Services will materially conform to the
applicable documentation.
7.2 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH HEREIN, THE SERVICES ARE PROVIDED "AS
IS" AND PROVIDER DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, WHETHER EXPRESS, IMPLIED,
OR STATUTORY, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE,
AND NON-INFRINGEMENT.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
8.1 EXCEPT FOR BREACHES OF SECTION 5 (CONFIDENTIALITY), CLIENT'S PAYMENT
OBLIGATIONS, OR EITHER PARTY'S INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS, NEITHER PARTY
WILL BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL
DAMAGES, AND EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT
WILL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CLIENT IN THE TWELVE (12)
MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

9. TERMINATION
9.1 Termination for Cause. Either Party may terminate this Agreement or an
Order Form if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and
fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days of written notice.
9.2 Termination for Convenience. Client may terminate an Order Form prior
to the end of its then-current Term for any reason upon thirty (30) days'
written notice to Provider. In such event, Client shall pay Provider an
early termination fee equal to fifty percent (50%) of the Fees that would
otherwise have been payable for the remainder of the then-current Term,
which the Parties agree is a reasonable estimate of Provider's
anticipated losses and not a penalty.
9.3 Upon any termination, Client shall pay all Fees accrued through the
effective date of termination, together with any early termination fee
due under Section 9.2.

10. INDEMNIFICATION
10.1 Provider will defend Client against third-party claims alleging that
the Services, as used in accordance with this Agreement, infringe a valid
U.S. patent or copyright, and will pay resulting damages finally awarded,
subject to Section 8.
10.2 Client will defend and indemnify Provider against third-party claims
arising from Client's data, Client's breach of this Agreement, or
Client's use of the Services in violation of applicable law.

11. GENERAL PROVISIONS
11.1 Independent Contractors. The Parties are independent contractors, and
nothing herein creates a partnership, joint venture, or agency
relationship.
11.2 Assignment. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the
other Party's prior written consent, except that either Party may assign
this Agreement in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of
substantially all of its assets.
11.3 Force Majeure. Neither Party will be liable for delays caused by
events beyond its reasonable control.
11.4 Notices. All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and
delivered by email (with confirmation of receipt), overnight courier, or
certified mail to the addresses set forth in the applicable Order Form or
such other address as a Party designates in writing. Notices are deemed
given upon confirmed receipt.
11.5 Entire Agreement; Order of Precedence. This Agreement, together with
all Order Forms and SOWs, constitutes the entire agreement between the
Parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements.
In the event of a conflict, the terms of an Order Form control over this
Agreement solely with respect to the Services described therein.
11.6 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State
of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.
11.7 Auto-Renewal. Each Order Form's Initial Term will automatically
renew for successive twelve (12)-month periods (each, a "Renewal Term,"
and together with the Initial Term, the "Term") unless either Party
provides written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to
the end of the then-current Term. Fee Adjustments applicable to any
Renewal Term are governed by Section 4.4.
11.8 Severability; Waiver. If any provision of this Agreement is held
unenforceable, the remaining provisions will remain in full force and
effect. No waiver of any provision will be effective unless in writing
and signed by the waiving Party.
11.9 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each
of which is deemed an original.
