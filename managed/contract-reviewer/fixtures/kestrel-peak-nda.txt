MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of
the date of last signature below (the "Effective Date") by and between
Kestrel Peak Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company with its
principal place of business at 1201 Third Avenue, Suite 2200, Seattle, WA
98101 ("Kestrel"), and Brightloom Data, Inc., a Delaware corporation with
its principal place of business at 228 Park Avenue South, PMB 100482, New
York, NY 10003 ("Brightloom"). Kestrel and Brightloom are each a "Party"
and collectively the "Parties."

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship
under which Brightloom may provide data services to Kestrel (the
"Purpose"); and

WHEREAS, in connection with the Purpose, each Party may disclose to the
other certain confidential and proprietary information;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed
by either Party (in such capacity, the "Disclosing Party") to the other
Party (in such capacity, the "Receiving Party"), whether disclosed orally,
in writing, or by inspection, including without limitation business plans,
financial information, product designs, technical data, source code,
customer lists, pricing, and the terms of this Agreement, together with
any notes, analyses, or materials prepared by the Receiving Party that
incorporate or are derived from the foregoing.
1.2 "Disclosing Party" and "Receiving Party" refer to whichever Party is
disclosing or receiving Confidential Information at a given time; a Party
may act as both Disclosing Party and Receiving Party under this Agreement.

2. OBLIGATIONS
2.1 The Receiving Party will hold the Disclosing Party's Confidential
Information in confidence and use at least the same degree of care it
uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, but
no less than reasonable care.
2.2 The Receiving Party will use Confidential Information solely for
purposes of evaluating and pursuing the Purpose, and will not disclose it
to any third party except to its employees, officers, and professional
advisors who have a need to know and are bound by confidentiality
obligations at least as protective as those set forth herein.

3. EXCLUSIONS
Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or
becomes generally available to the public through no fault of the
Receiving Party; (b) was rightfully in the Receiving Party's possession
prior to disclosure by the Disclosing Party, as shown by the Receiving
Party's contemporaneous written records; or (c) is rightfully obtained by
the Receiving Party from a third party without restriction on disclosure.

4. TERM
This Agreement is effective as of the Effective Date and continues for a
period of five (5) years (the "Term"). The confidentiality obligations set
forth in Section 2 apply throughout the Term and, upon expiration of the
Term, shall be of no further force or effect.

5. RESIDUALS
Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in this Agreement, either Party
may use for any purpose the Residuals resulting from access to or work
with the other Party's Confidential Information, provided that such Party
does not disclose the Confidential Information itself except as expressly
permitted herein. "Residuals" means information retained in the unaided
memory of persons who have had access to Confidential Information,
including ideas, concepts, know-how, or techniques contained therein,
whether or not such persons intentionally memorized the information.
Neither Party has any obligation to limit or restrict the assignment of
such persons or to pay royalties or provide any accounting for any work
resulting from the use of Residuals.

6. RETURN OF MATERIALS
Upon the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party will
promptly return or destroy all materials containing Confidential
Information, except that the Receiving Party may retain one copy in its
legal files solely to monitor compliance with this Agreement, and except
as retained pursuant to Section 5 or generated by automated backup
processes.

7. NO LICENSE; REMEDIES
7.1 Nothing in this Agreement grants either Party any license or
ownership interest in the other Party's Confidential Information,
patents, copyrights, or other intellectual property, except the limited
right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.
7.2 Recipient acknowledges that any actual or threatened breach of this
Agreement by Recipient will cause irreparable harm to Kestrel Peak
Holdings, LLC for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy,
and that Kestrel Peak Holdings, LLC shall be entitled, without the
necessity of posting bond or other security, to seek injunctive relief and
specific performance in a court of competent jurisdiction, in addition to
any other remedies available at law or in equity.

8. NO OBLIGATION
Nothing in this Agreement obligates either Party to disclose any
information, to proceed with the Purpose, or to enter into any further
agreement.

9. GENERAL PROVISIONS
9.1 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of
Washington, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.
9.2 Assignment. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other
Party's prior written consent, except in connection with a merger,
acquisition, or sale of substantially all of its assets.
9.3 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement
between the Parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior
discussions and agreements regarding the Purpose.
9.4 Severability; Waiver. If any provision of this Agreement is held
unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.
No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party.
9.5 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of
which is deemed an original.
